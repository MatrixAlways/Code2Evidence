        Agentic refinement: a small gain at substantial cost

        A target-blind Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking refinement loop improved Hamrick 3D-box mIoU from 59.86 ± 12.48% to 61.87 ± 7.01%. Refinement took 65 min 56 s; post-freeze evaluation took 12 s. This 10-video parsing pilot is separate from the current reasoning experiments.

        What the experiments tell us.

        Evidence quality

        Geometry is not dynamics.

        High box overlap does not validate identity continuity, contact timing, velocity or latent physical properties. Partial reference graphs are not complete physical Oracles.

        Evidence interface

        Meaning must be explicit.

        Bass improves when event occurrence is separated from finite-horizon absence. The same physical facts can be misunderstood when their semantics are compressed.

        Reasoning & transfer

        A local gain is not a general solution.

        Numerical timelines help selected conditional tasks; other studies still expose scale-polarity errors, unsupported assumptions and weak calibration.
